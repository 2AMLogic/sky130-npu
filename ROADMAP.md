# Roadmap

sky130-npu is an 8 x 8 INT8 multiply-accumulate array fed by four banked
OpenRAM SRAM macros on SkyWater sky130, built to stress macro placement and
routing congestion in the open flow driven by klayout-tools (`klt`).

Status as of 2026-11-13, `main` @ `bc53cd2`.

[WORK_PLAN.md](WORK_PLAN.md) is the label-generated queue; the graded verdict
of record is `manifests/sky130-npu.signoff.json`, tracked in #4.

**The whole remaining gap sits behind item 2.** Eleven records under
`layout/records/` (ten runs and one analysis) and a twelfth attempt in PR #111
all stop in global routing with `GRT-0116`; no routed GDS exists. The recorded
cause (`layout/records/20261111-macro-pin-geometry/`) is structural: the macro
puts port 0 data pins on its south edge and port 1's data output on its north
edge, both ports carry array traffic, so no orientation faces both at the
logic. Orientation, channel width, placement density and the sandwich
floorplan have been tried; halo fails `PDN-0217` (klayout-tools#5302).
